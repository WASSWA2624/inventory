# 011 — Accessibility rules

**Phase** 01 · Orchestration & working agreements  |  **Depends on** [005](005-reuse-policy.md)

## Goal

Bake accessibility into the catalogue rather than auditing it at the end.

## Files

- `dev-plan/01-orchestration/_accessibility.md` (new)

## Steps

1. Minimum touch target 48dp; every interactive element has a semantic label.
2. Text scales to 200 percent without clipping; colour is never the only status signal.
3. Primary actions sit within thumb reach; camera controls remain usable with gloves.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Design-system tasks include these checks in their acceptance criteria.
